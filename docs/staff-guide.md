# Using the stock system

**HappyMed Pharmacy — everything you need for a normal shift, on one page.**

---

## Open this address

```
happymed.vercel.app
```

Type it into Chrome or Safari, the same way you'd open Facebook. Save it as a bookmark so you only do this once.

Works on a phone, a tablet, or the counter computer. Same address on all of them.

---

## Signing in

1. Open the address above. You'll see a green **HappyMed** sign-in box.
2. Type the **email address** the owner gave you. It's yours alone — don't use someone else's.
3. Type your **password**, then tap **Sign in**.
4. The first time only, it asks you to **pick your own password**. Choose something you'll remember, at least 10 characters.

> **You'll stay signed in** for about a week, so you won't be typing your password every shift.
> On a shared counter computer, use **Sign out** in the menu at the top right when you finish.

## Find your role

Everyone has one of three roles. Your role decides what you see in the menu — so
**your screen will look different from your colleague's, and that's normal.**

Not sure which you are? Sign in and look at your menu. If you can see **Staff**,
you're the Owner. If you can see **Receive stock** but not Staff, you're a Pharmacist.
If you see neither, you're Counter staff.

---

### 🟩 Counter staff

**Your job: record everything that goes out, and flag anything that looks wrong.**

You see five things in the menu: Dashboard, Expiry alerts, Stock on hand, Dispense, Products.

**Every shift**

1. Sign in and look at the **Dashboard** first. It tells you what's low and what's expiring.
2. Use **Dispense** for every single sale — as it happens, not at the end of the day.
3. If anything shows **red**, take it off the shelf and tell the pharmacist.

**When a customer asks "do you have this?"**

Go to **Stock on hand** and type the name in the search box. It tells you how many you have without you walking to the shelf.

**What you can't do — and shouldn't try to work around**

You can't record deliveries, change product details, or correct mistakes. That's deliberate. If something's wrong, tell the pharmacist rather than finding another way to make the screen match the shelf.

---

### 🟦 Pharmacist

**Your job: everything counter staff do, plus deliveries, corrections and ordering.**

You see everything above, plus: **Receive stock**, **Suppliers**, **Purchase orders**, **Reports**.

**Every day**

1. Check the **Dashboard**.
2. **Receive stock** for any delivery that arrived — before the boxes go on the shelf, not later.
3. Fix anything counter staff reported. Go to **Stock on hand**, find the batch, and use the adjust option. Every correction asks for a reason — write a real one.

**Every week**

1. Open **Expiry alerts** and work down the orange list. Move those boxes to the front.
2. Anything red comes off the shelf and gets recorded as disposed — don't just bin it.
3. Check **Products** for anything below its reorder point, then raise a **Purchase order**.

**When the order arrives**

Open the purchase order and receive against it. The system tracks partly-delivered orders, so if the supplier sends 8 boxes out of 10, record the 8 — it stays open for the rest.

**What you can't do**

Add or remove staff accounts, change system settings, or view the audit log. Those are the owner's.

---

### 🟨 Owner

**Your job: accounts, settings, and keeping an eye on the whole thing.**

You see everything, plus: **Staff**, **Settings**, **Audit log**.

**Set up once**

1. **Settings** — set how many days ahead counts as "expiring soon" (orange) and "critical" (red). The defaults are 90 and 30 days. If your suppliers won't take returns inside 60 days, set it wider.
2. **Staff** — create an account for each person, choose their role, and give them a temporary password. They'll be asked to pick their own at first sign-in.

**Every week**

Open **Reports** to see what's moving, what's dead stock, and what you're losing to expiry.

**Every month**

1. Skim the **Audit log**. Every action in the system is there with a name and a time. You're looking for anything odd — corrections at strange hours, one person making a lot of adjustments.
2. Check the **Staff** list. Anyone who's left should be **deactivated**, not deleted — that keeps their history intact while blocking their login.

**When someone forgets their password**

**Staff** → find them → **Reset password**. Give them the new temporary one. This signs them out everywhere immediately.

## What's in the menu

On a phone, tap the **☰** button at the top left to open it.

| Menu item | What it's for | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | The morning summary. What's low, what's expiring, what needs attention. Start here. | Everyone |
| **Expiry alerts** | Everything approaching its expiry date, soonest first. | Everyone |
| **Stock on hand** | How much of everything you have. Search at the top to find one medicine. | Everyone |
| **Dispense** | Medicine going out to a customer. Record it here every time. | Everyone |
| **Products** | Every medicine you carry, and the details of each one. | Everyone |
| **Receive stock** | A delivery arriving from a supplier. | Pharmacist, Owner |
| **Suppliers** | Who you buy from, and their contact details. | Pharmacist, Owner |
| **Purchase orders** | Orders you've placed and what's still outstanding. | Pharmacist, Owner |
| **Reports** | What's selling, what's sitting, what's being lost to expiry. | Pharmacist, Owner |
| **Staff** | Accounts, roles and passwords. | Owner |
| **Settings** | Expiry warning periods and pharmacy details. | Owner |
| **Audit log** | Every action anyone has taken, with names and times. | Owner |

> **Missing a menu item?** Nothing is broken — see **Find your role** above.
> If you need something that isn't there, ask the owner.

## What the colours mean

| Colour | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🟢 **Green** | Fine | Plenty of time before it expires. Nothing to do. |
| 🟠 **Orange** | Expiring soon | Sell this one first. Don't order more of it yet. |
| 🔴 **Red** | Expired, or nearly | Take it off the shelf now and tell the pharmacist. |

These are the same colours everywhere in the system, so you can judge a whole screen at a glance without reading the dates.

## Giving medicine to a customer

*Everyone does this.*

1. Tap **Dispense**.
2. Start typing the medicine name and pick it from the list.
3. Enter how many. There are two boxes — whole packs, and loose pieces from an opened pack. Use whichever fits, or both.
4. The screen shows you **which lot number to take off the shelf**. Take that exact box — it's the one expiring soonest.
5. Tap **Dispense** to confirm.

> ⚠️ **Take the box it tells you** — not the one at the front of the shelf.
> The system picks the one closest to expiring so that stock gets used before it goes to waste.
> This is the single most important habit on this page.

---

## Taking in a delivery

*Pharmacist and Owner only.*

Do this **before** the boxes go on the shelf. A delivery that's on the shelf but not in the system makes every number wrong until someone notices.

1. Tap **Receive stock**.
2. Pick the medicine.
3. Copy the **lot number** and **expiry date** from the actual box — not from the invoice. The box is what you'll be holding later.
4. Enter how many arrived, in whole packs and loose pieces.
5. Enter what you paid per pack.
6. Tap **Receive**.

> **If the same lot number already exists with a different expiry date, the system will refuse.**
> That means one of the two was mistyped. Check the box again — don't force it through.

## When something looks wrong

**It says there isn't enough stock, but I can see the boxes**
Someone probably forgot to record a delivery. Tell the pharmacist — don't work around it.

**I recorded the wrong amount**
Don't try to undo it yourself. Tell the pharmacist, who can correct it properly. Every change is recorded, so nothing is ever lost.

**The page won't load**
Check your internet first — open any other website. If that works but this doesn't, tell the owner.

**It's asking me to sign in again**
Normal after about a week, or if you're on a different device. Just sign in again.

**I forgot my password**
The owner can reset it for you from the Staff page. There's no self-service reset.

---

## Three rules

- **Record it as it happens.** Not at the end of the day. If the system and the shelf disagree, everyone stops trusting the system.
- **Never share your login.** Every action is stamped with your name. If someone else uses your account, mistakes land on you.
- **Red means stop.** Expired medicine doesn't get sold, doesn't get "used up quickly", doesn't get sent home with anyone.

---

Questions your pharmacist can't answer go to the owner. Don't guess with stock records — asking takes a minute, fixing a wrong count takes an afternoon.
